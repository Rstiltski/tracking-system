# 🎯 What I Can Do - Habit Experiments Page

## Purpose
Run experiments on your habits to test changes and measure their impact. Scientific approach to habit optimization.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Create experiment** | Click "New Experiment" | Experiment setup form opens |
| **Start experiment** | Click "Start" | Begin tracking experiment |
| **End experiment** | Click "End" | Complete experiment and see results |
| **View results** | Click on completed experiment | See experiment analysis |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Experiment Information
- **Experiment Name**: What you're testing
- **Hypothesis**: What you think will happen
- **Duration**: How long the experiment runs
- **Status**: Active/Completed/Upcoming

### Experiment Results
- **Baseline**: Performance before experiment
- **Experiment Period**: Performance during experiment
- **Change**: Percentage improvement/decline
- **Conclusion**: Whether hypothesis was correct

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **One Variable**: Change only one thing per experiment
2. **Long Enough**: Run experiments for at least 2 weeks
3. **Document**: Record your hypothesis and results
4. **Learn**: Even failed experiments teach something

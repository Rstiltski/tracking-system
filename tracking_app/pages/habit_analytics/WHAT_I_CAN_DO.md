# 🎯 What I Can Do - Habit Analytics Page

## Purpose
View detailed analytics for individual habits. See completion patterns, best times, correlations, and predictions.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Select habit** | Choose from dropdown | View analytics for that habit |
| **View patterns** | Browse analytics sections | See various analyses |
| **Export data** | Click export button | Download habit data |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Completion Analytics
- **Completion Rate**: Overall percentage
- **Best Days**: Most productive days
- **Best Times**: Optimal completion times
- **Streak Analysis**: Streak history and patterns

### Predictions
- **Streak Forecast**: Predicted streak length
- **Completion Probability**: Likelihood of completing today
- **Risk Assessment**: Risk of breaking streak

### Correlations
- **Related Habits**: Habits that influence this one
- **Mood Correlation**: How habit affects mood
- **Time Correlation**: Best time relationships

# 🎯 What I Can Do - Template Sharing Page

## Purpose
Share your habit templates, goal templates, and routines with others. Import templates from the community.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Create template** | Click "Create Template" | Template from your data |
| **Share template** | Click "Share" | Make template public |
| **Import template** | Click "Import" on template | Add to your account |
| **Browse templates** | Search and filter | Find community templates |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Template Types
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Habit Sets** | Groups of habits |
| **Goal Templates** | Pre-made goals |
| **Routines** | Daily/weekly routines |
| **Challenges** | Custom challenges |

### Template Information
- **Name**: Template name
- **Description**: What it includes
- **Author**: Who created it
- **Downloads**: Popularity count
- **Rating**: User ratings

---

## 🔗 Navigation

### Where I Can Go From Here
- **Habits** (`habits.py`) - Apply habit templates
- **Goals** (`goals.py`) - Apply goal templates
- **Challenges** (`challenges.py`) - Use challenge templates

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Share Good Routines**: Help others with your successful setups
2. **Check Ratings**: Use highly-rated templates
3. **Customize**: Adapt templates to your needs
4. **Give Credit**: Rate and thank creators

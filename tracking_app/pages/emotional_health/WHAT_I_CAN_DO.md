# 🎯 What I Can Do - Emotional Health Page

## Purpose
Track your emotional well-being using the RGB emotional model. Log emotions, identify patterns, and understand your emotional health over time.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Log emotion** | Select RGB values, add notes | Emotion entry saved for today |
| **View history** | Scroll to history section | See past emotional entries |
| **View patterns** | Check charts | See emotional patterns over time |

### Forms I Can Fill

**Emotion Log Form:**
- **Red Channel**: Energy/Intensity level (0-255)
- **Green Channel**: Balance/Harmony level (0-255)
- **Blue Channel**: Calm/Peace level (0-255)
- **Notes**: What's affecting your emotions

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### RGB Emotion Model
| Channel | Represents | High Value |
|---------|------------|------------|
| **Red** | Energy, passion, intensity | Active, energized |
| **Green** | Balance, harmony, growth | Balanced, content |
| **Blue** | Calm, peace, reflection | Relaxed, peaceful |

### Emotion History
- **Color Swatch**: Visual representation of RGB values
- **Date**: When the entry was logged
- **Notes**: Context for the emotion

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Log Daily**: Track emotions consistently for patterns
2. **Use Notes**: Capture context for your emotional state
3. **Notice Patterns**: Look for triggers in your emotions
4. **Balance All Three**: Aim for balanced RGB for emotional health

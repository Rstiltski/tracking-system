# 🎯 What I Can Do - Challenges Page

## Purpose
Participate in challenges to push yourself further. Join community challenges or create personal challenges to motivate your progress.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Join challenge** | Click "Join" on challenge | Challenge added to your list |
| **Create challenge** | Fill form, click "Create" | New personal challenge created |
| **Track progress** | View challenge card | See your progress |
| **Leave challenge** | Click "Leave" button | Remove from active challenges |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Active Challenges
- **Challenge Name**: What the challenge is
- **Duration**: How long it lasts
- **Progress**: Current completion percentage
- **Start/End Date**: Challenge timeframe
- **Participants**: Number of people (if public)

### Challenge Types
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Habit Streak** | Maintain a streak for X days |
| **Completion** | Complete X habits/tasks |
| **Time-based** | Track X hours of activity |
| **Custom** | Your own challenge criteria |

### Your Challenge Stats
- **Active Challenges**: Current challenges
- **Completed**: Successfully finished
- **Failed**: Challenges not completed
- **Win Rate**: Success percentage

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Start Small**: Begin with shorter challenges
2. **Be Realistic**: Choose achievable goals
3. **Stay Consistent**: Daily progress adds up
4. **Join Public**: Community challenges add accountability

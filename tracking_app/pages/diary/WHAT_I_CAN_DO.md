# 🎯 What I Can Do - Diary Page

## Purpose
A personal diary for daily reflections and memories. Different from journal - focused on daily moments and quick entries.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Add diary entry** | Write in text area, save | Entry recorded for today |
| **View entries** | Scroll through list | See past diary entries |
| **Edit entry** | Click edit button | Modify existing entry |
| **Delete entry** | Click delete button | Remove entry |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Diary Entries
- **Date**: Entry date
- **Content**: Your diary text
- **Mood**: Mood indicator (if set)
- **Photos**: Attached images (if any)

### Calendar View
- Entries displayed on calendar
- Quick navigation to past dates

---

### Where I Can Go From Here

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Daily Habit**: Write something every day
2. **Keep It Simple**: Short entries count too
3. **Include Emotions**: Track how you felt
4. **Review Monthly**: Look back on your month

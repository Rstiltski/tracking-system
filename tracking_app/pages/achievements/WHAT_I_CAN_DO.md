# 🎯 What I Can Do - Achievements Page

## Purpose
View all your unlocked achievements, track progress toward new ones, and celebrate your milestones. The gamification center of the tracking system.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **View achievements** | Browse achievement list | See all available achievements |
| **Filter achievements** | Use filter dropdowns | Show specific categories or status |
| **View details** | Click on achievement | See unlock requirements and date |
| **Share achievement** | Click share button | Share unlocked achievement |

### Views Available

| Tab | What It Shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Unlocked** | Achievements you've earned |
| **Locked** | Achievements still to unlock |
| **Secret** | Hidden achievements (revealed when unlocked) |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Achievement Information
- **Icon**: Visual badge for the achievement
- **Name**: Achievement title
- **Description**: What the achievement is for
- **XP Reward**: Experience points earned
- **Tier**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, or Diamond
- **Unlocked Date**: When you earned it (if unlocked)

### Achievement Categories
| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Habits** | Habit-related achievements |
| **Tasks** | Task completion achievements |
| **Goals** | Goal achievement milestones |
| **Streaks** | Streak-based achievements |
| **Special** | Hidden/secret achievements |

### Summary Stats
- **Total Unlocked**: Number of achievements earned
- **Total XP**: XP from achievements
- **Completion %**: Percentage of all achievements unlocked

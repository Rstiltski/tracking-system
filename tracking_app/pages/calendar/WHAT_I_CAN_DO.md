# 🎯 What I Can Do - Calendar Page

## Purpose
View your habit completions, tasks, and goals on a monthly calendar. Visualize your activity patterns and navigate through your tracking history.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Navigate months** | Click ← → arrows | View different months |
| **View day details** | Click on a date | See activities for that day |
| **View heatmap** | Scroll to heatmap | See completion patterns |

### Navigation Controls
- **← Previous**: Go to previous month
- **→ Next**: Go to next month
- **Today**: Jump to current month

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Calendar View
- **Month Grid**: Days of the month in calendar format
- **Completion Colors**: Color-coded based on completion rate
- **Today Highlight**: Current date emphasized
- **Activity Indicators**: Icons for habits, tasks, goals

### Heatmap View
- **GitHub-style heatmap**: Visual representation of activity
- **Color intensity**: More activity = darker color
- **Date range**: Last 12 months

### Day Detail (when clicked)
- **Habits completed**: List of habits done that day
- **Tasks completed**: Tasks finished that day
- **Goals progress**: Any goal updates that day
- **Notes**: Any journal entries

---

## 🔗 Navigation

### Where I Can Go From Here
- **Habits** - Manage habits
- **Tasks** - View tasks
- **Goals** - Track goals
- **Weekly** (`weekly.py`) - Weekly summary view

---

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Spot Patterns**: Look for weekly patterns in your activity
2. **Check Streaks**: See your consistency visually
3. **Review Past**: Navigate to past months to review progress
4. **Plan Ahead**: Use calendar view to plan upcoming activities

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟢 Green | High completion rate |
| 🟡 Yellow | Partial completion |
| 🔴 Red | Low completion |
| ⬜ Gray | No activity |

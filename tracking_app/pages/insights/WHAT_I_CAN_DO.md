# 🎯 What I Can Do - Insights Page

## Purpose
View AI-powered insights and correlations from your tracking data. Discover patterns between habits, health, mood, and productivity.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **View insights** | Browse insight cards | See AI-generated insights |
| **Filter insights** | Use category filters | Show specific insight types |
| **Dismiss insight** | Click dismiss | Remove insight from view |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Correlation Insights
- **Habit-Mood Correlations**: Which habits affect your mood
- **Sleep-Productivity**: How sleep impacts your task completion
- **Exercise-Energy**: Relationship between exercise and energy levels

### Pattern Detection
- **Best Days**: Which days you perform best
- **Peak Hours**: When you're most productive
- **Streak Predictions**: Likelihood of maintaining streaks

### Recommendations
- **Suggested Habits**: Habits that might benefit you
- **Optimal Times**: Best times for activities
- **Goal Suggestions**: Goals based on your patterns

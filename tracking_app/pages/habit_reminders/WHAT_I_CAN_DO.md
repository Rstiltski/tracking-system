# 🎯 What I Can Do - Habit Reminders Page

## Purpose
Set up reminders for your habits. Get notified when it's time to complete your habits.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **Create reminder** | Click "Add Reminder" | Reminder setup form opens |
| **Edit reminder** | Click edit on reminder | Modify reminder settings |
| **Delete reminder** | Click delete on reminder | Reminder removed |
| **Toggle reminder** | Click toggle switch | Enable/disable reminder |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Reminder Settings
- **Habit**: Which habit the reminder is for
- **Time**: When to send reminder
- **Frequency**: Daily, specific days, or custom
- **Status**: Active/Inactive

### Reminder Types
- **Push Notification**: Browser notification
- **Sound**: Audio alert
- **Email**: Email reminder (if configured)

---

## 🔗 Navigation

### Where I Can Go From Here
- **Habits** (`habits.py`) - Manage habits
- **Notification Settings** (`notification_settings.py`) - Global settings

---

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Set Realistic Times**: Pick times you can actually do the habit
2. **Don't Overdo It**: Too many reminders become noise
3. **Use Sound**: Audio alerts are harder to miss
4. **Adjust as Needed**: Change times if they're not working

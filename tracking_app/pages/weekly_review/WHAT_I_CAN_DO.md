# 🎯 What I Can Do - Weekly Review Page

## Purpose
Conduct a comprehensive weekly review of your progress. Reflect on achievements, identify improvements, and plan for the upcoming week.

---

## ✅ User Interactions

### Actions I Can Perform

| Action | How | Result |
|--------|-----|--------|
| **View weekly summary** | Scroll through page | See week's performance |
| **Write reflection** | Fill reflection text area | Save weekly reflection |
| **Set next week goals** | Fill goals section | Plan upcoming week |
| **Mark review complete** | Click "Complete Review" | Review saved |

---

## 📊 Data Displayed

### Weekly Summary
- **Habits Completed**: Total and percentage
- **Tasks Completed**: Total and breakdown by priority
- **Goals Progress**: Movement toward goals
- **XP Earned**: Experience points this week

### Review Sections
| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **What Went Well** | Celebrate successes |
| **What to Improve** | Identify growth areas |
| **Next Week Goals** | Plan ahead |
| **Habit Adjustments** | Modify habits if needed |

### Insights
- **Top Habit**: Best performing habit
- **Needs Attention**: Habits that struggled
- **Streak Status**: Current streaks
- **Achievement Progress**: Toward next achievement

## ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Schedule It**: Do your review at the same time each week
2. **Be Honest**: Reflect truthfully on performance
3. **Celebrate Wins**: Acknowledge your achievements
4. **Set Realistic Goals**: Plan achievable next week goals
5. **Take Your Time**: A good review takes 15-30 minutes

---

## 📝 Review Template

Each weekly review includes:
1. Summary of the week's data
2. Reflection prompts
3. Planning for next week
4. Optional habit adjustments
